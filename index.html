Cosmetic Grading & Quality Control

Standard supply grade is Grade A — approx. 90% new cosmetic condition. Grade B and C are offered on request at lower prices. Grading affects cosmetics and price only — every grade is 100% function-tested, and every unit passes five checks before shipment.

Grade CObvious wearDents, heavy wear or faded housing; functions verified — usually bought as spare units, donor units for repair, or for inconspicuous positions

The five checks every unit passes

  • Unbind original account: remove previous Hik-Connect / cloud binding, with screenshot or recording proof
  • Factory reset: confirm device back to unactivated state
  • Re-activate with strong password: no default credentials left
  • Full function test: image, IR night vision, motion, audio, network — checked one by one
  • QC label photo: unit and QC label shot together, sent before dispatch

Typical buyers include security installers, small system integrators, importers of second-hand CCTV equipment, and maintenance teams replacing failed units in existing systems. Grade choice is usually driven by how visible the mounting position is — see Commercial Terms for how grade affects pricing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers on the points that matter most when buying refurbished CCTV equipment across borders.

Are these cameras new or used?

They are used, refurbished cameras — not brand new. We are not an authorized Hikvision channel and the units carry no original-manufacturer warranty. Original brand logo and model labels are kept as-is. See Commercial Terms.

What does Grade A mean for refurbished Hikvision cameras?

Grade A is our standard supply grade: approximately 90% new cosmetic condition — minor signs of use, no visible scratches at 1 m, clean housing. Grading affects cosmetics and price only; every grade is 100% function-tested. See Cosmetic Grading & QC.

Will these cameras work with third-party NVRs?

Yes. Both models support ONVIF Profile S together with ISAPI, SDK and GB28181, so they connect to the majority of third-party recorders. For a mixed system, send us the NVR model and firmware region and we confirm compatibility before dispatch.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Samples are 1–2 pcs with international shipping included. Bulk FOB orders start from 50 pcs, and models and power versions (DC12V or PoE) may be mixed in one order.

What are the payment terms?

T/T — 30% deposit to confirm the order, 70% balance against a copy of the bill of lading. You pay the balance only after the goods are on board and the B/L copy is released to you; the original B/L or telex release is issued once the balance is received.

What after-sales support is provided?

Units are supplied with a defective-rate refund rather than physical replacement, because cross-border returns are costly for both sides. Report defects by photo plus serial number within 7 days of arrival; the refund is calculated on the confirmed defect rate of the batch, as agreed per order.

Can you source other Hikvision models besides these two?

Yes. These two are our core stock, but we can usually source most Hikvision DS-IPC and DS-2CD series units, including domes, PTZ cameras and NVRs. Message us the model you need and we check availability.
